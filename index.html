Si el 10 nos elige, debe ser por algo.

If the number 10 picks us, there has to be a reason. It is the line the shop has used since day one, and it is still the whole argument.

You are not booking a celebrity. You are booking the barber who cuts them, on a normal Wednesday, for the price of a normal haircut.

Nico Jaffe left Uruguay, built this shop in Wynwood, and now flies out to cut some of the most photographed heads in football. Between those trips the same clippers, the same technique and the same four barbers are here on 20th Street.

No membership, no consultation fee, no upsell ladder. Book, sit, get the cut that shows up on a Champions League broadcast.

  • Four documented footballers. Messi, Chelo Weigandt, Lamine Yamal and Rodrygo Goes. Every one of them posted, dated and public.
  • A signed shirt wall. The Almada 23 and the Argentina kit are hanging on the wall behind the chairs. Come see them.
  • Three countries on staff. Argentine, Uruguayan and Colombian barbers, so somebody here cuts hair the way you grew up having it cut.
  • A lounge, not a waiting room. Drinks, a pool table and a room built to sit in. Bring somebody.
  • We film cuts. The Star VIP package includes a reel of your cut, shot and edited here.
